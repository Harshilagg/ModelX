6bWhere it lives, and how much brutalism to take
Entry points
01 · PROFILEA fifth row in the spec sheet: "Comp card — 70% ready". The completeness number is the nudge that fills empty profiles. 02 · AFTER APPLY"Attach your comp card" on the apply confirmation. Brands receive the card inside the application, so the tool has a job to do on day one. 03 · ONBOARDINGLast onboarding step, skippable: "Make your card now." Free and unwatermarked is the reason to sign up at all. 04 · THE LOOPCard footer carries a QR to the live profile. Cards travel to outside castings; scans arrive back as brand views, and the model sees the count.
Brutalism: half Take: square corners, 3px ink borders, flat blocks, exposed mono labels, oversized condensed type. This screen uses all five. Leave: offset drop shadows, clashing primaries, tilted stickers, raw HTML-looking forms. Models are judged on their photos; loud frames cheapen them, and brands paying ₹75,000 need this to read professional. Where it goes: comp card tool, splash, onboarding, empty states. Feed, messages and job detail stay calm.

5dHow Home behaves — the reasoning
Home = board,
then feed
01 · ON OPENThe board answers the only urgent question: what's next, where, and am I booked. A countdown replaces the date so the answer needs no arithmetic. 02 · COLLAPSETap the header and the board folds to one line — the next call only. Feed-first people get their scroll back; the board never disappears. 03 · FEEDTwo-column masonry mixing shots and notes, so text posts stop looking like broken photo posts. All / Shots / Notes filters the column without leaving Home. 04 · NEW POSTSA flip tile counts unseen posts instead of pushing the feed down with a banner. 05 · EXITSDeparture row → job detail. All departures → tracker. Post → post detail. Sparkle → upload. One action per destination, nothing competing.
HELD BACK Marigold is the only accent here — periwinkle appears once, on a community note, and never on the board. Flip tiles stay on times and statuses.
